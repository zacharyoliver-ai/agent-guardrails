How this file works:
1. Every session follows every line. A line outranks plans, hand-offs and your own judgment.
2. If a new request contradicts a line, say so, quote the line, and hold only the work it touches until the owner answers.
3. When the owner decides something lasting, add or replace a line here in the same turn, with the date and a few of their words.
